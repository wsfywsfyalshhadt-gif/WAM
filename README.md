## Core Framework Modules

### 1. Cosmological Genesis & The Primordial Quark Nucleus ($\mathcal{Q}_{raw}$)
* **Key Concepts:**
  * **Pre-Big Bang State:** Models the pre-Big Bang universe as a unified, zero-volume raw matter nucleus ($\mathcal{Q}_{raw}$) with non-existent spatial dimensions and unified forces[span_24](start_span)[span_24](end_span).
  * **Dissociation & Phase Transition:** Modeled via the critical energy state ($E_{BB}$)[span_25](start_span)[span_25](end_span):
    $$\mathcal{Q}_{raw} \xrightarrow{E_{BB}} \sum_{i=1}^{n} q_i + \sum_{j=1}^{m} e_j^-$$
  * **Lepton Derivation:** Treats electrons as physical derivatives of primordial raw quark mass with respect to expansion energy[span_26](start_span)[span_26](end_span):
    $$e^- = \frac{\partial(\mathcal{Q}_{raw})}{\partial(E_{BB})}$$
  * **Quark-Gluon Plasma (QGP):** Describes post-dissociation "Cosmic Smoke" thermodynamics, Stefan-Boltzmann energy density ($\rho_{QGP}$), and Friedmann expansion dynamics[span_27](start_span)[span_27](end_span).

### 2. Quantum Electrodynamics (QED) Decoupling at the Event Horizon
* **Key Concepts:**
  * **Event Horizon Darkness ($r = r_s$):** Formulates event horizon darkness via QED decoupling and vanishing material electron charge density ($N_e \rightarrow 0$) rather than mechanical photon trapping[span_28](start_span)[span_28](end_span)[span_29](start_span)[span_29](end_span)[span_30](start_span)[span_30](end_span).
  * **Decoupling Dynamics:** As $r \rightarrow r_s^+$, electron density and charge current vanish ($\langle J^\mu \rangle \rightarrow 0$), causing the electromagnetic interaction term to vanish ($\mathcal{L}_{int} = 0$)[span_31](start_span)[span_31](end_span)[span_32](start_span)[span_32](end_span).
  * **Quantum Vacuum State Transition:** Transitions the energy-momentum tensor expectation value to a renormalized pure quantum vacuum state[span_33](start_span)[span_33](end_span)[span_34](start_span)[span_34](end_span):
    $$\lim_{r \rightarrow r_s^+} \langle T_{\mu\nu} \rangle = \langle 0 \vert \hat{T}_{\mu\nu}^{(\text{vacuum})} \vert 0 \rangle_{\text{Renorm}}$$

### 3. The Dual Tensor Theory of Mass Density
* **Key Concepts:**
  * **Dual Role of Mass Density ($\rho_m$):** Acts macroscopically to govern spacetime curvature ($G_{\mu\nu}$) and microscopically to generate internal sectional pressure ($P$)[span_35](start_span)[span_35](end_span)[span_36](start_span)[span_36](end_span)[span_37](start_span)[span_37](end_span).
  * **Action & Hydrostatic Equilibrium:** Derived from a modified Einstein-Hilbert action and stress-energy conservation ($\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu} = 0$), yielding central pressure scaling $P_c \propto \rho_m^{4/3}$[span_38](start_span)[span_38](end_span)[span_39](start_span)[span_39](end_span)[span_40](start_span)[span_40](end_span).
  * **Critical Density Threshold ($\rho_{crit}$):** Defines the density condition required to overcome Coulomb repulsion and ignite nuclear fusion[span_41](start_span)[span_41](end_span)[span_42](start_span)[span_42](end_span)[span_43](start_span)[span_43](end_span):
    $$\rho_{crit} \ge \left( \frac{\xi E_c}{C_1 \mu m_u G M^{2/3}} \right)^3$$
  * **Secondary Energy Emission:** Establishes fusion reaction rates ($R_{12}$) and radiative luminosity ($L = \frac{dE}{dt} = c^2 \frac{d(\Delta m)}{dt}$) as secondary byproducts[span_44](start_span)[span_44](end_span)[span_45](start_span)[span_45](end_span).

### 4. Two-Stage Internal Quantum Stripping Model

  * **Stage 1 (Spatial Collapse):** Neutralization of quark fractional charges ($q_f \rightarrow 0$) causes spatial interaction terms ($\mathcal{L}_{ext}$) to vanish and microscopic spatial volume to collapse ($V \rightarrow 0$)[span_46](start_span)[span_46](end_span)[span_47](start_span)[span_47](end_span)[span_48](start_span)[span_48](end_span).
